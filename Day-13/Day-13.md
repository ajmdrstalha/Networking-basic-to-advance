## 📘 Day 13 – Wireless Networking Basics

### 🧩 1. What is Wireless Networking?

**Wireless Networking** allows devices to communicate **without physical cables** using radio waves.

- Provides mobility and flexibility  
- Commonly used in homes, offices, and public places  
- Requires access points (AP) or wireless routers  

Example:
- Wi-Fi network at home or coffee shop  
- Wireless printer or IoT device connection  

---

### 🔢 2. Wireless Standards (Wi-Fi)

| Standard | Frequency | Max Speed | Range |
|----------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| 802.11a | 5 GHz | 54 Mbps | 35 m |
| 802.11b | 2.4 GHz | 11 Mbps | 38 m |
| 802.11g | 2.4 GHz | 54 Mbps | 38 m |
| 802.11n | 2.4/5 GHz | 600 Mbps | 70 m |
| 802.11ac | 5 GHz | 1.3 Gbps | 35 m |
| 802.11ax | 2.4/5/6 GHz | 10 Gbps | 100 m |

---

### 🔁 3. Wireless Network Types

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| WLAN | Wireless LAN, covers small area like home/office |
| WPAN | Personal network, e.g., Bluetooth devices |
| WMAN | Metropolitan network, e.g., city-wide Wi-Fi |
| WWAN | Wide area network, e.g., 4G/5G cellular |

---

### ⚙️ 4. Wireless Security

- **WEP:** Older, weak encryption  
- **WPA:** Improved security over WEP  
- **WPA2:** Strong encryption (AES)  
- **WPA3:** Latest standard, enhanced security  
- **SSID:** Name of the wireless network  
- **MAC Filtering:** Restricts devices by MAC addresses  

---

### 🗂️ 5. Practical / Lab Practice

- Scan nearby Wi-Fi networks using your device  
- Connect to a secured network and check signal strength  
- Change SSID and password on a wireless router  
- Enable WPA2/WPA3 encryption for security  

---

### 🧠 6. Key Terms

- **Access Point (AP):** Device providing wireless connectivity  
- **SSID:** Network name broadcasted by AP  
- **Encryption:** Secures wireless communication  
- **Channel:** Frequency range used for Wi-Fi  
- **Roaming:** Moving between APs without losing connection
